# HyperFrames Caption Trial

## Style Prompt

A warm, restrained hotel film with editorial Chinese typography. Keep the room image recognizable. Let the words move with the slow, deliberate rhythm of a premium stay: one soft fold-in, a short hold, then a second phrase that arrives in a clean cascade. The typography should feel composed and tactile, with ample negative space.

## Colors

- Deep walnut scrim: `#211914`
- Warm ivory: `#F7F0E5`
- Champagne accent: `#D9BD8C`
- Soft shadow: `rgba(20, 15, 12, 0.45)`

## Typography

- Chinese display: `Songti SC`, regular weight
- Chinese supporting text: `Hiragino Sans GB`, light weight
- Small English eyebrow: `Hiragino Sans GB`, uppercase with wide tracking

## Motion

- Fold the hero phrase in on its baseline with a soft perspective turn.
- Reveal Chinese characters in a short stagger.
- Hold each phrase long enough to read; use one restrained crossfade between phrases.
- Keep the room image on a very slow push-in.

## What NOT to Do

- No neon, hard black outlines, bouncing karaoke pills, or rapid shake.
- Do not cover the bed or center of the room with dense text.
- Do not use several accent colors or decorative sound effects.
